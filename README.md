# Big Brother
*Big Brother is watching you!* This Fabric mod removes Mojang's ability to block specific servers that are in violation of the Minecraft EULA.

## Copyright warning
This mod is in total compliance with the copyright of Minecraft and does not contain any piece or portion of code from Minecraft. You have no reason to take it down besides the fact that you want to control who can play a block game.

## My opinion on blocked servers
While I don't support servers that offer pay-to-win mechanics, gambling, and other shady monetization tactics, I do have a real reason to make this mod. Mojang not-so-recently introduced new rules that force ungoverned, First Amendment servers to limit what can be said, therefore taking away your freedom of speech.

It's gone as far as 2B2T, where you can't even type swear words without being censored. Anyway, if you don't like the malicious servers that try to steal your money I mentioned above, just don't play on them. Simple as.

## Configuration
No need to configure anything! This mod is very simple and does not have any unnecessary features. Just enjoy your regained freedom!
